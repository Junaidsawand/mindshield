## 3. Mandatory Chapter Structure

The template prescribes exactly **five chapters**:

| Chapter | Template Title | Heading Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Chapter 1** | INTRODUCTION | Heading 1 (two lines: "CHAPTER 1" then "INTRODUCTION") |
| **Chapter 2** | LITERATURE REVIEW/BACKGROUND | Heading 1 (two lines) |
| **Chapter 3** | RESEARCH METHODOLOGY | Heading 1 (two lines) |
| **Chapter 4** | RESULTS AND DISCUSSION | Heading 1 (two lines) |
| **Chapter 5** | CONCLUSION AND FUTURE DIRECTIONS | Heading 1 (two lines) |

Followed by:

| Section | Style |
|---|---|
| **REFERENCES** | Heading 1 |

### 3.1 Chapter Title Format
Each chapter opens with TWO consecutive Heading 1 paragraphs:
- Line 1: `CHAPTER N` (e.g., "CHAPTER 1")
- Line 2: Chapter title in ALL CAPS (e.g., "INTRODUCTION")

### 3.2 Heading Hierarchy
| Level | Style | Numbering | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter title | Heading 1 | CHAPTER N | CHAPTER 1 |
| Section | Heading 2 | N.N | 1.1 Motivation |
| Subsection | Heading 3 | N.N.N | 1.2.1 Subheading |

### 3.3 Required Chapter Sections
The template shows each chapter ending with a **Summary** section (e.g., "2.4 Summary", "4.4 Summary", "5.4 Summary").

## 4. Table and Figure Conventions

### 4.1 Table Numbering
- Format: `Table {chapter}-{sequence}` (e.g., "Table 2-1", "Table 4-1")
- Caption style: **No Spacing** style
- Caption position: **Above** the table
- Example: `Table 2-1 Table of Numbers`

### 4.2 Figure Numbering
- Format: `Figure {chapter}-{sequence}` (e.g., "Figure 2-1", "Figure 4-1")
- Caption style: **Figure Caption** style (11pt)
- Caption position: **Below** the figure
- Example: `Figure 2-1 Figure Caption (Use figure caption style)`

### 4.3 List of Tables / List of Figures
- Both use the **table of figures** Word style (10pt)
- Auto-generated from captions

---

## 5. Reference Format

- Style: IEEE numbered format `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`
- In-text citation: `[1-3]` for ranges
- Reference list section: Heading 1, titled "REFERENCES" (template spells it "REFRENCES" — likely a typo)
- Ordering: Sequential numbered list
- Example entries follow IEEE conference/journal format

3. Are appendices expected? If so, how should they be numbered (Appendix A, B, C)?
4. Should chapters start on new pages (page break before each chapter)?
5. Is there a minimum or maximum total page count?

7. Is color printing allowed for figures and charts?
8. Should the "REFERENCES" heading spelling be corrected from the template's "REFRENCES"?
9. Are page numbers required in the header, footer, or both?
10. Is the certificate/approval page signed before or after binding?
